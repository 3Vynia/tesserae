# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

People who enjoy collecting and caring for things: players of collectible card games who are tired
of pay-to-win loops, and readers who like myths and want to meet less familiar figures. They play
in short sessions on a phone or a laptop, often in the evening, and come back for the collection
more than for any single battle.

## Product Purpose

Tesserae is a free collectible card game where every card is a figure from ancient myth. Players
earn cards by playing, raise them, dress them up and send them into automatic battles. Success is a
player who opens the collection just to look at it, and who learns something true about a myth
along the way.

## Positioning

Nothing is for sale. Drop rates are always visible and pity is generous. The game makes no network
requests at all and keeps progress in the browser. Every card cites the classical source of its
story.

## Operating Context

- Runs entirely offline in the browser; the save can be exported and imported as a file.
- Sessions are short: check daily quests, pull a few times, run a battle, admire the collection.
- English is the source language, Spanish ships alongside it, and more languages can be added with
  files alone, so every layout must tolerate longer text.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Collection shown as a mosaic over a night sky, grouped by pantheon.
- Summoning with banners, visible rates, pity and history.
- Automatic turn-based battles with teams of four or five, formation, synergies and elements.
- Levels 1 to 60, ascension, stars from duplicates, relics, cosmetic personalization.
- No monetization, no analytics, no third-party requests, no fonts or code from a CDN.
- Only public domain myth and folklore with no living worship; no figures from franchises or
  modern retellings.
- There is no final card art yet; placeholder art is generated and swappable.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Tesserae, the small tiles of a mosaic.
- Look: dark ink and midnight blue, gold leaf, parchment and constellations. The collection is a
  mosaic of tiles that light up over a night sky. Each pantheon has its own ornament (Greek meander,
  Norse knotwork, Egyptian lotus, Chinese clouds, Japanese waves and so on).
- Serif typography with character, open licensed and bundled.
- Motion is a core feature: showpiece moments (pulls, reveals, battles, the mosaic) get rich motion;
  everyday actions stay fast and light.
- The interface must have its own identity and must not borrow the look of any other game.
- Voice: plain, warm and a little wry; it respects the myths and the player's time.

## Evidence on Hand

- No card art, no testimonials, no player data. Nothing of that kind may be invented.
- Sources for every card are classical texts (Iliad, Poetic Edda, Book of the Dead, Kojiki and so
  on), cited on the card back.

## Product Principles

1. Everything is earned by playing; the game never asks for money or attention it did not earn.
2. The collection is the heart: looking at it should feel like standing under a lit vault.
3. Spend spectacle where it matters (a pull, a reveal, a victory) and keep the rest quick.
4. True to the sources: when unsure about a myth, say less.
5. Works for everyone: keyboard, screen readers, reduced motion, long translations, small phones.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Full keyboard play. Card information is available as text to screen readers.
`prefers-reduced-motion` gets a calm alternative (crossfades, static shine), not a broken page.
